Privacy policy

pagePackage Chrome extension · Effective 21 November 2026

In short: pagePackage works on your computer. It does not collect your browsing history, the pages you scan or the files you download, and it has no ads or analytics. The only personal information involved is the email address you give when you buy Pro, which is handled by our payment providers, ExtensionPay and Stripe.

Who we are

pagePackage is made by Pylerium ("we"). You can contact us at danqhu3@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The following is stored in your browser's extension storage on your own computer, and is never sent to us:

  • Your settings (folders, file naming, filters, theme and so on).
  • Recent scan results, so the gallery can show them again.
  • Your download queue and download history, including any tags and collections you add in the Library.
  • The websites you add to the Resource sites list.

You can delete this data at any time in Settings → Data & backup, or by removing the extension.

Monthly usage counts

The free plan includes monthly allowances (for example, 10 deep scans a month). To keep count, pagePackage stores how many of each you've used this month. These are numbers only. If Chrome sync is turned on in your browser, Google syncs these counts across your own signed-in Chrome browsers. They contain no information about what you scanned or downloaded.

Pages and files you choose

When you scan a page or download files, pagePackage reads that page and fetches the files you selected, directly between your browser and the website, the same as when you view them. Optional features work the same way: the network sniffer notes media addresses a page loads, deep scan fetches linked pages on the same site, and "watch page" re-scans a tab you chose when it reloads. Conversions and metadata removal happen entirely inside your browser. None of this is sent to us or anyone else.

Payments (Pro)

If you buy pagePackage Pro, the payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. You enter your email address and card details on their pages, and card details never reach the extension or us. ExtensionPay keeps your email address and plan so your purchase can be restored on another browser, and we can see the email address and plan of paying customers so we can provide support and refunds.

To know whether Pro is active, the extension checks your plan with ExtensionPay when it starts and about every six hours. It also reads the current date from a web server's response header (extensionpay.com or google.com), so monthly allowances follow the real calendar. These requests contain no browsing data.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your browsing history or the content of pages you visit.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • We don't sell or share data with anyone, and we don't use it for anything unrelated to pagePackage's purpose.

The use of information received by pagePackage adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Children

pagePackage is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it on this page and change the effective date above.
