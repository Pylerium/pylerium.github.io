Every image, video & file on a page. One gallery. One click.

pagePackage is a Chrome extension that finds everything a page contains, shows it in a gallery you can filter and preview, and saves what you pick: one by one, all at once, converted, or as a single ZIP.

Everything you need to package a page

Built for designers, researchers and creators who collect from the web every day.

Finds everythingImages, video, audio, documents, fonts, design files, 3D models and streams, including files hidden in code and lazy-loaded galleries.
Full-size originalsSwaps thumbnails for the original images on hundreds of sites, so downloads look sharp.
Batch download & ZIPDownload everything at once into tidy folders, or save a whole page as one ZIP.
Convert in your browserWebP, AVIF and SVG to PNG or JPG, video to MP4, audio from any video to MP3. Nothing is uploaded.
See and strip metadataView camera, lens, colour profile and GPS location, and remove them before you share.
Built for sessionsScan every open tab into one gallery, watch pages for new files, and keep a searchable library.
