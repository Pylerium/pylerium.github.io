Hypnagogia

Overview

A companion platform. You design a living plush companion in the Atelier, and every other
feature is a module that uses that companion profile: conversation, voice, dreamscapes, and
whatever gets added next. Text-to-speech is one capability, provided by
speak (Kokoro on MediaPipe, GPU).
