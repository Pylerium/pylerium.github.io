Privacy

Pylerium Manager · Effective 22 November 2026

The app keeps your data on your computer

Your apps, settings, keys and tokens are stored in your user profile on your own computer. Pylerium Manager doesn’t send them to us. It talks directly to the services you connect yourself (GitHub, and optionally Stripe, Cloudflare, Discord and Gmail) using your own accounts.

What the app sends to us

  • Update check: it downloads latest.json from this website to see if there’s a new version.
  • Licence check: with a subscription, it sends only your subscription id to our licence service, which replies whether it’s paid and until when. No name, email or usage data is sent.
  • Refund list: it downloads a list of cancelled licence ids from this website.

There are no analytics, trackers or ads in the app or on this page.

When you subscribe

Payment is handled by Stripe. We receive your name, email address, country and payment status (never your full card number) and use them only to send your licence key and receipts, provide support, keep tax records and handle refunds. Stripe’s own privacy policy applies to the payment itself.
