Subscriptions & refunds

Pylerium Manager Pro · Effective 22 November 2026

Try before you pay: every feature works free for 14 days, no card needed. If you subscribe and it isn’t right for you, ask within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund.

The subscription

Pylerium Manager Pro is A$19.99 a month (including any applicable tax), billed by Stripe on the same day each month. One subscription covers you on all of your own computers and includes every update.

Your licence key

After your first payment we email you a licence key. Paste it into Settings → Licence once. Pylerium Manager checks your subscription automatically, so you never need a new key when it renews.

Cancelling

Cancel any time from the link in your Stripe receipt, or in Pylerium Manager under Settings → Licence → Manage subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the month you’ve paid for and won’t be charged again. Your website stays online and everything you made stays on your computer; you just can’t publish changes until you subscribe again.

If a payment fails

Stripe retries the payment and emails you. Pro keeps working for 3 days past the end of your paid month while you update your card.

Refunds

Within 14 days of your first payment: a full refund, no questions asked. Later payments aren’t refunded part-way through a month, but cancelling stops the next one. Email danqhu3@gmail.com from the address you paid with. Refunds go back to your card through Stripe and usually appear within 5–10 business days.

Your consumer rights

This policy doesn’t limit any rights you have under the Australian Consumer Law or the consumer laws of your country.
