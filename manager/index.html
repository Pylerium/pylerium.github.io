Questions

Good to know

Do I need to know how to code?

No. You fill in forms and press Publish. Pylerium Manager writes the website, handles GitHub and keeps everything in sync.

Where does my data live?

On your computer. Your keys and settings never leave it, except for what you choose to publish (your website, to your own GitHub account) and the services you connect yourself, like Stripe, Discord, Gmail and, for paid downloads, your own Cloudflare account.

Does hosting cost anything?

No. GitHub Pages hosts your site for free and Cloudflare’s free plan covers paid-download delivery. Stripe only charges its usual fee when you make a sale.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. GitHub Pages supports custom domains; point yours at your github.io site and it keeps working with Pylerium Manager.

What happens when the trial ends?

Your website stays online. You just can’t publish changes or sell downloads until you subscribe, and everything you made is still there when you do.

Do I need a new key every month?

No. You paste your licence key once. Pylerium Manager checks your subscription by itself, and it keeps working offline until the end of the month you’ve paid for.

How do I cancel or change my card?

Use the link in your Stripe receipt, or Settings → Licence → Manage subscription in the app. You keep Pro until the end of the month you’ve paid for.

How do updates work?

Download the new version and replace the folder. Your apps, settings and keys are stored separately, so nothing is lost. The app tells you when a new version is out, and your subscription includes every update.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of your first payment, no questions asked. Email us and we’ll sort it out. Full policy.
